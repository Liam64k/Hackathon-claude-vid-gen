# Looks (visual styles)

Read only the section for the look the user chose. Scenes still use the Winter Lake pack unless an
art pack for that look exists; change captions, stamps, callouts, cards, HUD, accents, motion, SFX.

## Flat 2D
- Palette: Winter Lake pack as-is; accents #2F80ED blue, #F2C94C amber, #EB5757 danger, ink #1B2430.
- Shapes: clean vector fills, no outlines, soft drop shadows, rounded corners 16-24px.
- Motion: smooth ease-out 8-12 frames in, gentle spring settle; camera drifts slowly.
- Captions: system/Inter 700, white with dark pill backdrop, active word in amber.
- Stamps/callouts/cards: flat rounded panels, thin icon, one accent colour each; HUD minimal.
- Tone: calm, clear explainer; facts first, short sentences.
- Avoid: textures, outlines, sound-words on screen.

## Cartoon
- Palette: saturated brights over the pack: #FF6B35 orange, #FFD23F yellow, #3BCEAC teal, ink #1A1A2E.
- Shapes: thick 6-8px dark outlines on overlays, bouncy blobs, chunky rounded cards.
- Motion: squash-and-stretch pops, overshoot springs (back-out), 6-8 frame snaps, little wiggles.
- Captions: Bricolage Grotesque 800, white with 6px dark stroke, word pops scale 1.15 → 1.
- Stamps/callouts/cards: tilted stickers, starburst stamps, speech-bubble callouts with tails.
- Sound-words OK sparingly ("WHOOSH", "CRACK") on big beats, max one per 10 s.
- Tone: upbeat, playful, a little cheeky; jokes never undercut safety steps.
- Avoid: gore gags, mocking victims, comedy on real casualty facts.

## Paper cut-out
- Palette: muted craft tones: #E8DCC4 kraft, #C1440E rust, #4A6C6F slate, #2D2A26 ink.
- Shapes: overlays as torn-paper cards, slight jagged edges, layered paper drop shadows.
- Motion: stop-motion feel (hold every 2 frames), small random rotations ±2°, slide-ins with settle.
- Captions: Fraunces 600, dark ink on cream paper strip, slight tilt per line.
- Stamps/callouts/cards: rubber-stamp ink marks, pinned notes, tape strips on cards.
- Tone: warm storyteller, "let me show you", gentle and reassuring.
- Avoid: glossy gradients, neon, fast whip moves.

## Comic ink
- Palette: #FFE600 yellow, #E4002B red, #0057B8 blue, #111111 ink, halftone dots on panels.
- Shapes: bold black ink borders 5-7px, panel frames, speed lines, halftone shading on cards.
- Motion: hard cuts and punch-ins, 4-6 frame slams, quick shake on impacts, freeze-frame holds.
- Captions: Bangers, uppercase, yellow box with black border like comic narration boxes.
- Stamps/callouts/cards: jagged burst stamps, shout-bubble callouts, "MEANWHILE..." caption cards.
- Sound-words welcome on impacts ("BOOM", "SLAM") but never over safety instructions.
- Tone: punchy, dramatic, high stakes (great for zombie fiction); steps stay calm and exact.
- Avoid: blood, weapon close-ups, violent sound-words on people.

## Chalkboard
- Palette: board #2B3A2F, chalk white #F4F1E8, chalk yellow #F6E27A, chalk pink #F28FAD.
- Shapes: sketchy hand-drawn strokes, wobbly underlines, arrows and circled numbers, dust texture.
- Motion: write-on reveals (stroke draw 15-25 frames), linear-ish, eraser-wipe transitions.
- Captions: Caveat 700, chalk white, faint smudge; key word underlined in yellow.
- Stamps/callouts/cards: chalk boxes, hand-drawn checkmarks, numbered lists drawn on.
- Tone: friendly teacher, step-by-step lesson, "remember these three things".
- Avoid: drop shadows, glossy UI, comic sound-words.
